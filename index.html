Firefly Night

Night falls inside your room. Fireflies wake up on your table, your couch, your walls — and hide behind them. Catch them gently before dawn.

  • ✋ Reach out gently with your hands or controllers to catch a firefly. Swipe too fast and it startles away.
  • 🔔 Pinch or pull the trigger to send a chime rippling across your real room — hidden fireflies glow through your furniture and answer from where they hide.
  • ✦ Golden fireflies never sit still. Worth five.
  • 🌙 Your room remembers. At dawn the fireflies you kept settle on your walls — and they'll still be there next time.

Made for Meta Quest 3 / 3S in Quest Browser. Run Space Setup so your furniture is known, clear some floor, and allow “spatial data” when asked. Headphones optional — fireflies sing in 3D. A night lasts three minutes.

Desktop preview · drag to look · WASD to walk · click a firefly to catch · Space / right-click to chime · hold Shift to cradle · Esc to leave
